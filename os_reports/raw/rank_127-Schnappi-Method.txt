Data
The submission was built on three subsets of the OpenADMET PXR Challenge dataset (huggingface.co/datasets/openadmet/pxr-challenge-train-test).
single_concentration~21,000 rows, log2_fc_estimate
train 4,139 rows, pEC50 from 8-point dose–response curves (PXR-expressing cells)
test 513 rows—Prediction target

Method
A two-stage transfer-learning pipeline with ChemProp v2 (a directed message-passing neural network, D-MPNN, over molecular graphs):
Stage 1 — Self-supervised-style pretraining on the 21k single-concentration data.
The DMPNN backbone was trained to regress log2_fc_estimate from SMILES. This stage does not learn pEC50 values; its sole purpose is to expose the backbone to the PXR-relevant chemical space and learn a structure → activation-signal prior. The resulting checkpoint was saved as models/chemprop_pretrained_sc/model_0/best.pt.
Stage 2 — Fine-tuning on the 4,139 dose–response pEC50 labels.
The pretrained checkpoint was loaded and fine-tuned with the regression head pointing at pEC50.
Inference. The 5-replicate ensemble was applied to the 513 blinded test SMILES, the per-molecule mean was taken as the final pEC50, and the output was written in the required submission format (Molecule Name, SMILES, pEC50).
